# Portfolio Positions — Concise System Summary

## Core Principle
Treat each position as a defined risk unit, not a standalone idea.

## Position Risk Rule
- Risk per position: 3-5% of account value.
- Example: $50,000 account -> $1,500 max risk per trade at 3%.
- Size contracts from max loss, not from premium collected.

## Diversification Rule
- Diversify by independent drivers (sector/theme), not by ticker count.
- Avoid clustering risk in one industry (for example, multiple semiconductor names at once).

## Expiration Ladder Rule
- Stagger expirations (for example, 30/45/60 DTE) to avoid concentrated rollover and event risk.
- Keep capital recycling continuous rather than synchronized.

## Aggression Tiers
- Core: tighter hedge width, lower risk, higher consistency.
- Standard: baseline hedge width.
- Aggressive: wider hedge width, higher variance, used selectively.

## Portfolio Risk Cap
- Total open max loss across all positions: 20-30% of account.
- This is the portfolio kill switch for new entries.

## Market-Regime Positioning
- Strong trend: allow more positions and moderate aggression.
- Choppy/uncertain: reduce count and tighten hedges.
- Stress/crash conditions: pause new risk and prioritize defense.

## Exit and Adjustment Rules
- Take profits early around 50-75% of max gain.
- Cut or adjust at thesis break or around 50-70% of max loss.
- Avoid holding by default to expiration when risk/reward has degraded.

## Intel Examples (INTC)
- Example account: $50,000; per-trade risk target at 3% = $1,500.
- Standard INTC structure example: synthetic at 121 with protective put at 110.
- Estimated max loss per 1-lot: about $1,100-$1,500, so position size remains 1 contract under the 3% rule.

### Intel Aggression Tiers
- Core: 121/115 hedge width (tighter protection, lower variance).
- Standard: 121/110 hedge width (balanced risk/reward).
- Aggressive: 121/95 hedge width (wider protection, higher variance).

### Intel Expiration Ladder Example
- INTC position A: 30 DTE (core tier).
- INTC position B: 45 DTE (standard tier).
- INTC position C: 60 DTE (aggressive tier, smaller size or less frequent use).

### Intel Management Example
- Take profit: close around 50-75% of projected max gain.
- Cut/adjust: close or roll if thesis fails, or if loss reaches about 50-70% of max risk.
- Portfolio control: keep aggregate open max loss across all positions within 20-30% account cap.

## Bottom Line
Scale by controlling per-trade risk, limiting total portfolio risk, diversifying true exposures, and enforcing disciplined exits.
