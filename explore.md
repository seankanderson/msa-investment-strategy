## 1. Bull Put Spread

*(Your short‑put DNA, professionally constrained)*

### Core Idea

You are selling downside fear **with guardrails**.

**Structure**

*   Sell put (the one you *would* normally sell)
*   Buy a lower‑strike put as insurance

This is not a different trade — it’s a **capital‑efficient version of the same opinion**.

***

### How to Structure It *Your Way*

Use these anchors:

*   **Short strike:**
    *   Delta: \~20–30
    *   Level: Under a clear support, VWAP range, or prior base
*   **Width:**
    *   $5–$10 on liquid underlyings
    *   Wide enough that max loss is tolerable, not emotionally loud
*   **Credit target:**
    *   ≥ 25–35% of width
    *   Example: $5 wide → $1.25–$1.75 credit

***

### Why This Fits You

*   Same thesis as selling puts: *“This level should hold”*
*   No assignment management
*   Frees capital to deploy **multiple income trades**
*   You already know how to manage the short leg — the long leg just caps pain

***

### Management Rules (Keep It Simple)

*   **50–70% max profit → exit**
*   **If price touches short strike early:**
    *   Roll *once* in time
    *   If roll credit is poor → exit, redeploy elsewhere
*   **Do not turn into a project**

> This trade should feel boring when it’s working.

## 3. Short Put + Long Call

*(Income first, optional asymmetric upside)*

Think of this as a **directionally biased income trade**, not a “big upside play.”

***

### Structure

*   Sell OTM put (your income engine)
*   Buy cheap, further‑out OTM call

This is **not** a debit gamble — the call must be:

*   Cheap
*   Small
*   Emotionally irrelevant if it expires worthless

***

### Defined‑Risk Version (Recommended)

To stay aligned with your risk philosophy:

➡️ **Bull Put Spread + Long Call**

Downside is capped, upside is *open-ish*.

***

### When This Trade Shines

*   Strong uptrend
*   Shallow pullbacks
*   IV not crushed
*   Assets you *like* but don’t want to chase with shares

***

### Practical Construction

*   **Put:**
    *   20–30 delta
    *   30–60 DTE
*   **Call:**
    *   10–15 delta
    *   Same expiration or longer
    *   Cost ≤ 20–30% of put credit

If put credit = $1.50  
Call should be \~$0.30 or less.

***

### Why This Fits Your DNA

*   The put **pays you to be patient**
*   The call rewards unexpected trend continuation
*   You are not relying on the call — it’s optional convexity

> You’re not paying for a dream — you’re tipping the upside.

***

### Failure Mode (Accept It Up Front)

*   Call dies quietly in chop
*   Put still does the heavy lifting

That’s fine. That’s expected.

## 5. Diagonal Put Spread

*(Time is the edge, not direction)*

This is your **“professional volatility rental” trade**.

***

### Structure

*   Sell short‑dated put
*   Buy longer‑dated, lower‑strike put

You profit from:

*   Faster decay in the front month
*   Optionality in the back month

***

### When to Use This

*   Front‑month IV inflated
*   Earnings *just passed* or are distant
*   You want income **without binary exposure**
*   Sideways‑to‑slightly‑bullish outlook

***

### Your Optimal Setup

*   **Short put:**
    *   7–21 DTE
    *   20–25 delta
*   **Long put:**
    *   45–90 DTE
    *   Same strike or 1–2 strikes lower

You want the **short premium to decay quickly** while the long put barely moves.

***

### Why This Fits You

*   Still short premium
*   Defined worst‑case outcome
*   You can:
    *   Roll short leg repeatedly
    *   Convert into other structures if needed
    *   Exit cleanly if thesis breaks

***

### Management Rules

*   Front‑month put hits **50–70% profit** → buy it back
*   Re‑sell a new short put if structure still makes sense
*   If price drops hard:
    *   Long put cushions impact
    *   You *don’t* panic‑adjust

***
