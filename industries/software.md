# Software (Enterprise, Platforms, AI)

**Macro Profile:** Growth sector with high sensitivity to interest rates. Software stocks are long-duration assets — their value is concentrated in future earnings, which makes them vulnerable to rate-hike cycles (2022 was a textbook example: the XLK/IGV collapsed as the Fed hiked 500bps). Outperforms in mid-expansion when rates are low and corporate IT budgets are growing. The AI secular tailwind partially decouples the sector from the pure macro cycle — cloud and AI infrastructure spending has continued even in a challenging rate environment.

**Key Cycle Drivers:** Corporate IT budget cycles, interest rates (discount rate for future cash flows), enterprise hiring/headcount (SaaS seat licensing), cloud migration penetration rate, AI capital spending commitments.

**Rate Sensitivity:** High. Growth software names with high P/E multiples compress meaningfully when rates rise. Enterprise software with high current FCF (MSFT, ORCL, CRM) is more resilient than high-multiple unprofitable growth names.

## Options Trading Notes

**IV Characteristics:** High-growth names (PLTR, DDOG, CRWD) carry significantly elevated IV year-round. Enterprise stalwarts (MSFT, ORCL, CRM) have moderate IV with pronounced spikes on earnings. Turnaround names (ZM, DOCU, TWLO) carry IV that has not yet compressed to their current business stability — creating selling opportunities.

**Best Premium-Selling Setups:**
- MSFT is one of the best CSP candidates in the entire market: household name, massive FCF, AI tailwind, dividend support, and sufficient liquidity in the options chain across all expirations. Sell 30-45 DTE puts on market-wide corrections.
- IBM carries above-average IV relative to its bond-like characteristics — sell puts or the wheel on pullbacks while collecting the ~3% dividend.
- ZM and DOCU are FCF machines trading at compressed multiples. Their IV is still elevated from their high-growth era, making premium selling on these names unusually attractive.
- Avoid selling naked puts on high-multiple names like NOW or CRWD — a modest multiple compression can produce large dollar moves even without business deterioration.

**Cycle Timing:** Software is best entered (via put-selling or long stock) during rate-peak environments when multiples have already compressed and the forward outlook for cuts improves. Avoid adding premium exposure to high-multiple names in mid-rate-hike cycles.
