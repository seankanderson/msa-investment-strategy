# Real Estate (REITs)

**Macro Profile:** The most rate-sensitive sector in the equity market. REITs are required to distribute 90% of taxable income as dividends, making them yield instruments that compete directly with bonds for investor capital. When rates rise, REIT multiples compress regardless of property fundamentals. When rates fall or peak, REITs tend to rally sharply as the discount rate applied to their income streams falls and their yield premium over Treasuries becomes attractive again. Within the sector there is significant dispersion: Industrial REITs (PLD) and data center REITs (EQIX) benefit from secular demand that partially offsets rate headwinds; mortgage REITs (AGNC) are pure rate plays — leveraged portfolios of agency MBS that expand or compress with rate spreads; healthcare REITs (WELL) are driven by demographics.

**Key Cycle Drivers:** Federal Funds Rate direction, 10-year Treasury yield, real estate vacancy rates by property type, same-store NOI growth, cap rate spreads, supply pipeline for each property subtype, Fed rate normalization pace.

**Rate Sensitivity:** Extremely high. This is the most rate-sensitive sector. The correlation between REIT performance and the 10-year Treasury yield is among the highest of any equity sector. Falling rates are the single most reliable REIT catalyst.

## Options Trading Notes

**IV Characteristics:** Moderate IV across most REITs with sharp spikes on Fed meeting days (interest rate sensitivity creates event risk around FOMC statements and dots projections). Mortgage REITs (AGNC, MPW) carry higher IV for their price. Data center REITs (EQIX) carry growth-stock-level IV for the sector.

**Best Premium-Selling Setups:**
- O (Realty Income) is a textbook defensive put-selling vehicle: pays a monthly dividend, investment-grade tenant base, 50+ year dividend growth record, and IV that spikes on rate fear regardless of business quality. Sell CSPs 5-8% OTM on any 10-year Treasury spike that drags the sector down.
- VICI is a high-quality wheel candidate: triple-net gaming leases are effectively unbreakable in normal economic conditions, dividend yield is strong, and IV is elevated from REIT sector correlation rather than business risk.
- AGNC is the highest-IV name in the table relative to price. Its ~$10 share price and high options premium relative to underlying make it appealing for premium selling, but risk management requires understanding that book value can compress rapidly if long-end rates spike.
- Avoid naked puts on MPW — the dividend was already cut once and tenant concentration risk is real. Use defined-risk spreads if participating.

**Cycle Timing:** The ideal REIT entry signal is when the 10-year Treasury yield peaks and the Fed signals the end of a hiking cycle. REITs historically front-run rate cuts by 6-12 months. The worst time to enter REITs is when the Fed is actively hiking and inflation is above target — the double compression of rising cap rates and rising discount rates creates sustained multiple pressure.
