# Consumer Discretionary

**Macro Profile:** The most economically sensitive consumer sector. Discretionary spending — restaurants, retail, travel, entertainment, automobiles — is the first thing consumers cut when income or confidence declines, and the first to recover when conditions improve. This makes the sector a leading indicator of the economic cycle. The best entry points are near recession troughs when pessimism is maximum, unemployment is peaking, and the Fed has begun cutting rates. Within the sector there is significant dispersion: Amazon and Booking Holdings are near-monopoly platforms with pricing power; auto companies (F, GM) are deeply cyclical; luxury and off-price retail tend to hold up better than mid-market.

**Key Cycle Drivers:** Consumer confidence index, unemployment rate, real wage growth, mortgage rates (housing-related discretionary), auto loan rates, credit card delinquency rates, travel demand, restaurant same-store sales comps.

**Rate Sensitivity:** High. Mortgage rates directly affect home improvement spending (HD, LOW). Auto loan rates affect new vehicle sales (F, GM). Consumer credit costs affect general discretionary spending. Lower rates are broadly positive for the sector.

## Options Trading Notes

**IV Characteristics:** Wide range. Amazon (AMZN) has moderate IV relative to the dollar premium on its options due to high share price. Auto names (F, GM) carry elevated IV from cyclical and EV transition uncertainty. Turnaround names (NKE, SBUX) carry higher IV than their brand quality would suggest — a premium-selling advantage. Booking Holdings and ORLY have meaningful earnings IV spikes.

**Best Premium-Selling Setups:**
- NKE is an excellent wheel candidate: iconic global brand, new CEO catalyst, IV elevated from execution uncertainty but the long-term brand is not impaired. Sell 30-45 DTE puts after each down leg. Assignment is acceptable given the brand's recovery trajectory.
- SBUX is similar — Niccol's operational reset is the kind of predictable recovery that rewards patient premium sellers. IV has been elevated since the CEO change; sell puts at support levels near the 52-week low range.
- F has very high IV for a large-cap stock, creating exceptional premium income relative to the dollar exposure. The dividend is strong and buybacks are ongoing. Assign into a wheel position.
- MCD and YUM are quasi-defensive within the sector (royalty/franchise models). Lower IV than retail, appropriate for conservative CSP strategies.
- TJX is a recession-resistant play within discretionary — IV is lower than peers, sell CSPs on broad retail sector selloffs when TJX is caught in the downdraft despite its structural advantages.

**Cycle Timing:** Start building CSP exposure when consumer confidence indexes hit multi-year lows and the Fed begins cutting rates. The sector moves well in advance of reported economic improvement because it is forward-looking. Reduce exposure and consider closing positions when unemployment claims fall below 200k and consumer confidence rebounds to multi-year highs.
